Privacy Policy

BabySteps is a private family space for memories, photos, videos, diary entries and practical care records. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how families can control or delete their data.

1. Who we are

BabySteps is a private family memories and care app operated by its developer, Miguel Ángel Batista Ruiz.

For privacy or support questions, contact us at mabatista2010@me.com.

2. Data we collect

Depending on how you use BabySteps, we may collect and process:

  • Account data: email address, authentication identifiers, display name, language preference and profile avatar.
  • Family and child data: family spaces, roles, invitations, child names or nicknames, birth or pregnancy dates, child avatars and access permissions.
  • User content: memories, milestones, photos, videos, audio, album items, diary entries, private notes, reactions and related metadata.
  • Private care records: growth, feeding, sleep, teeth and vaccine records entered by family administrators.
  • Notification data: push notification tokens, notification preferences and delivery status where notifications are enabled.
  • Technical data: device/app information, security logs, error information and operational records needed to keep the service reliable and secure.
  • Support communications: information you send when requesting help.

3. How we use data

We use data to:

  • create and secure user accounts;
  • sync private family memories and care records across devices;
  • manage invitations, roles and access permissions;
  • store and display photos, videos, audio and other family content;
  • send optional service notifications;
  • provide support and troubleshoot technical issues;
  • prevent misuse, protect accounts and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use BabySteps content for third-party advertising, and we do not sell personal data.

4. Sharing and service providers

BabySteps is not a public social network. Content is shared only within the family spaces and permissions configured in the app.

We rely on trusted technical providers to operate the app, including backend, authentication, database, storage, hosting, build, analytics-free diagnostics and push notification infrastructure. These providers process data only as needed to provide the service.

The current backend is powered by Supabase, with project infrastructure hosted in the European region configured for BabySteps. Push notifications may involve Apple Push Notification service and Google/Expo notification infrastructure depending on the platform.

5. Children and family data

BabySteps may contain information about children because parents or responsible adults choose to create a private family space. Children do not need to create accounts to use BabySteps.

Parents and family administrators are responsible for choosing what information they add, which relatives they invite and what content is visible to each person.

6. Retention and account deletion

We keep data while your account or family space is active, or as long as needed to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes or comply with legal obligations.

You can request deletion directly in the app from Settings → Privacy and session → Delete my account. BabySteps requires your current password and a confirmation phrase to prevent accidental deletion.

  • If you are the only active administrator of a family, deleting your account deletes the family space and its associated children, memories, album, diary, care records, notes, reactions and media.
  • If other active administrators remain, BabySteps keeps the shared family space and removes your own account, profile, membership and content you created or uploaded.
  • If you are a guest, BabySteps removes your account, profile, access, private notes and reactions without deleting the administrator's family content.

Some limited technical logs or backups may remain for a short period as part of provider security and operational systems, but they are not used as active app content.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise these rights, contact us at mabatista2010@me.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Security

BabySteps uses authentication, private storage, database access rules and role-based permissions to protect family content. We design the app around private family sharing rather than public discovery.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account or family content has been exposed, contact us as soon as possible.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as BabySteps evolves. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the update date shown at the top.
